Turn on Auto-submit?

Proposals will be sent without your approval, inside your caps: 15 a day, 3 an hour, 10 minutes apart, during active hours only. We recommend switching after about two weeks in Review with no warnings from Upwork.

Auto-submit also runs faster: Upwork is scanned every 2 minutes, each proposal goes out as soon as it is written, and the newest job goes first.

If Upwork ever stops allowing automatic confirmation, the tool switches itself back to Review and tells you.

Review queue

What happens to each job

  1. Find. Upwork is searched for your keywords every few minutes.
  2. Filter. Country, budget, rate, proposal count, then an AI match score.
  3. Write. Claude writes the opening for this job; the rest of your template stays as is.
  4. Loom. Your video is copied and renamed to the job title.
  5. Review. It waits here for you, or goes straight out in Auto-submit.
  6. Send. Approved proposals go out one at a time, spaced apart, in active hours.
  7. Log. Result, Connects and Loom link are saved; daily report by email.

Guardrails: 15 a day · 3 an hour · 10 min apart · stop below 20 Connects · auto-stop after 3 errors.

Sending

When this is on, approved proposals are submitted to Upwork one at a time, highest match first, and spend Rafa's Connects. Off: nothing is sent, proposals only wait in the queue.

Before turning sending on: review proposals for a full day in Review first, and make sure Rafa's account has Connects.
