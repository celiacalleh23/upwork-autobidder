What happens to each job

  1. Add. Sync. Jobs are automatically pulled in from our external provider.
  2. Filter. Country, budget, rate, proposal count, then an AI match score.
  3. Write. Claude writes the opening for this job; the rest of your template stays as is.
  4. Loom. Your video is copied and renamed to the job title.
  5. Review. It waits here for you, or goes straight out in Auto-submit.
  6. Send. Approved proposals go out one at a time, spaced apart, in active hours.
  7. Log. Result, Connects and Loom link are saved; daily report by email.

Guardrails: 15 a day · 3 an hour · 10 min apart · stop below 20 Connects · auto-stop after 3 errors.
